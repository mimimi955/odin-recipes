Beef Wellington

Go back to homepage

Ingredients

Easy to make in about 30 minutes. The best Beef Wellington in the world!

Ingredients

  • Beef tenderloin
  • Cloves
  • Potatoes
  • Onions
  • Garlic

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Cook the potatoes and onions in a large pot.
  3. In a small pan, heat the cloves and garlic.
  4. Season the beef tenderloin with salt and pepper.
  5. Wrap the beef in the potato mixture.
  6. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 130°F (54°C).
