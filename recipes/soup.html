Ingredients

Easy to make in about 30 minutes. The best soup in the world!

Ingredients

  • Chicken broth
  • Carrots
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Potatoes

Steps

  1. Heat the chicken broth in a large pot.
  2. Add the carrots, onions, and garlic.
  3. Cook for 10 minutes.
  4. Add the potatoes and cook until tender.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
